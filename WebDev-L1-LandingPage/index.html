Where it comes from

Three farms. Three stories.

Every bag carries the name of the farm, the altitude it grew at, and the hands that picked it. No blends, no mystery sourcing — just coffee you can trace back to soil.

IN

Chikmagalur, India

1,100m · Arabica · Washed

Grown in the shade of pepper vines on the Baba Budangiri hills, where coffee first arrived in India four centuries ago. Notes of jaggery and orange peel.

ET

Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia

1,900m · Heirloom · Natural

Sun-dried on raised beds for eighteen days. Floral, winey, unmistakably itself — the coffee most roasters chase but rarely find.

CO

Huila, Colombia

1,700m · Caturra · Honey

A cooperative of eleven smallholders on volcanic soil. Balanced body, notes of stone fruit and dark caramel.
